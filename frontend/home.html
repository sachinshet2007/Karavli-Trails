About Karavali Trails

Karavali Trails is an eco-tourism trip planner designed to help travellers explore the beautiful destinations of Uttara Kannada, Karnataka. Discover breathtaking beaches, magnificent waterfalls, green forests, historical temples and local attractions. Plan personalized trips, find comfortable stays, explore local restaurants and connect with transportation services, bike rentals and tourist guides through one convenient platform.

Travel lightly, leave it greener

  • Carry a reusable bottle and take your plastic back with you.
  • Stay on marked trails in forests and keep a distance from wildlife.
  • Respect temple customs and ask before photographing people.
  • Prefer local guides, homestays and family-run eateries.
